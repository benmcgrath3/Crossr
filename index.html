      About this crossword

      How to play

      • Click or tap a square or clue, then enter letters. Click the selected square again to switch direction.
      • Arrow keys move around the grid and skip black squares.
      • Space or Enter switches Across/Down. Tab and Shift + Tab move between clues.
      • Backspace erases. Escape takes you to the puzzle menu.
      • Use the menu for pencil mode, checking, revealing, and restarting.
      • On a phone or tablet, use the letter keyboard below the grid.

      Progress is stored in this browser, without an account. It does not sync across devices.
